Delete your Oasis account

Oasis · developed by Pete Hon

In the app

  1. Open Oasis and sign in.
  2. Tap the account icon at the top right.
  3. Tap Delete account, enter your password and confirm.

By email

If you can no longer use the app, send an email to petehon1205@gmail.com from the address you registered with, asking for your account to be deleted. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and password).
  • Your traveler profile, including your vehicle photo and phone number.
  • Your water place, including its photo and business details.
  • All refill requests you sent or received, including the copies of your profile attached to them.
  • Your favorite water places and any problem reports you sent.
  • The push notification addresses of your devices.

Deletion is permanent and happens immediately after you confirm. Nothing is kept, apart from technical backups of the hosting provider, which are overwritten within a few weeks.
