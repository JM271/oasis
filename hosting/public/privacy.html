Privacy policy

Oasis · last updated 1 November 2026

This policy explains which personal data the Oasis app processes, why, and what rights you have. It applies under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who is responsible

Pete Hon, developer of Oasis. Contact: petehon1205@gmail.com

What we process and why

  • Account: your email address and password, to sign you in. Passwords are stored only in encrypted form by Firebase Authentication.
  • Traveler profile: name, vehicle, and optionally phone number, a short text about you and a photo of your vehicle. Only the hosts you send a refill request to can see it.
  • Water place: if you offer water, its name, address and map position, opening times, price, suitable vehicles, and optionally a photo, your business details and website. This is visible to all users of the app.
  • Refill requests: the amount of water, your message, its status, the time you announced your arrival, and a copy of your traveler profile. Visible to you and the host.
  • Favorites: the water places you save as favorites. Only you can see them.
  • Reports: if you report a problem with a water place, the place, the reason and your account ID, so we can check the place. Reports are not shown to the host or to other users.
  • Location: your device location, only when you use “Near me” or “On my route”, place the pin of your water place, or save a new pin (the app then checks that you are at the place). It is used for distances, routes and this check and is not stored by us; only the pin you save becomes part of your water place.
  • Push notifications: if you allow notifications, an address for your device and the app's language, so we can tell you about new requests, the host's answer and a traveler's arrival.
  • Photos: photos you choose are reduced in size on your phone and stored with your profile or water place.

The legal basis is the provision of the service you request (contract) and, for location and notifications, your consent, which you can withdraw in your device settings at any time.

Oasis contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and we do not sell or rent your data. Payments are arranged directly between travelers and hosts; the app processes no payments.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC): sign-in, database (stored in the EU), server functions and push notifications.
  • Google Maps Platform, Places API: address search on Android. Your search text is sent to Google.
  • OSRM (router.project-osrm.org): driving routes and distances. Your location, your destination and the positions of nearby water places are sent to it.
  • OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org): map images. Your device's IP address is visible to the map server.
  • Photon by komoot (photon.komoot.io): address search outside Android. Your search text is sent to it.

Some of these providers process data outside Switzerland and the EU, including in the USA, under the safeguards they provide (such as standard contractual clauses or the Swiss-US and EU-US Data Privacy Frameworks).

How long we keep data

Your data is kept until you delete it in the app or delete your account. Deleting your account removes your account, profile, water place, requests, favorites, reports and device addresses immediately; see Delete your account.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, or a copy of your data, and you can object to processing. Most of this you can do directly in the app; otherwise email petehon1205@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or your local data protection authority.

Children

Oasis is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update this page and the date at the top.
